About Antony

Antony Robinson has been in ministry for years. In 2025, he still didn't know who he was.

That year, Antony found himself borrowing money just to stay afloat. He was losing money faster than he could explain it. His wife had to step in and provide for their family in ways he never wanted her to have to.

And underneath all of it was a quieter, harder truth: after years of ministry — years of preaching, of titles, of doing the work — Antony still didn't actually know who he was.

The Pattern

Looking back, the pattern is clear.

It didn't start in 2025. Back in 2010, Antony stepped into entrepreneurship, believing that was who he was. He built his sense of self around that title — and carried all the stress and strain that comes with trying to live up to something made into an identity instead of a calling.

In 2016, a mentor — a prophet in his life — spoke into him and identified a prophetic calling on his life. He began pursuing that calling, joined Christ First Community Church, got licensed that same year, and was ordained as a minister in 2019.

But even with the titles — licensed, ordained, in ministry — Antony was still lost. He was performing an identity instead of resting in one. He hadn't yet learned the difference.

Between 2021 and 2023, he stopped preaching altogether. Not because he lost his faith, but because he didn't feel good enough to keep going.

“I couldn't do what I was created to do, because I hadn't yet become who I was created to be.”

The Turning Point

Then, in 2025, something started to shift.

Antony started running that year — not for fitness, but as a kind of meditation. In that quiet, repetitive space, God began revealing his true identity in Christ, along with gifts he already had — communication, writing, a word of knowledge, a word of wisdom — gifts he had never believed he deserved.

He started receiving clear directives to start a ministry. And he resisted, doubting he was good enough for that too.

That resistance held until September 2025, when his father prophesied over him and confirmed the call. That confirmation was the turning point.

Where That Led

Becoming before doing — finally lived out.

Since then, Antony has stepped fully into the identity Christ had already given him. Out of that confirmation, he founded Disciples First Ministries — a ministry built to awaken believers to their identity in Christ and help them walk as true sons and daughters of God.

That same shift brought new clarity to Be More 2 Do More, which Antony had actually started back in 2023 with his brother-in-law, Henry Harden III — he just hadn't yet understood what it was really for.

And The Eager Heart became the written testimony of this whole journey — proof, on paper, that he couldn't do what he was created to do until he became who he was created to be.

Your Next Step

If you've been doing the work without ever becoming who you were created to be, start by naming what's driving it.
