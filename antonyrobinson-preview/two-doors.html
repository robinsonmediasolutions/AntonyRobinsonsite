Two Doors

One mission, two audiences: those seeking growth in their field, and those seeking growth in their faith.

Both come from the same conviction — that who you become determines what you build. Choose the door that fits where you are right now.

For Growth in Your Field

Be More 2 Do More

"Become who you're created to be, do what you're created to do."

Be More 2 Do More is a personal growth company for leaders and entrepreneurs. Through coaching, a guided identity assessment, and books built on the Eager Heart Alignment Framework, it helps driven people move from burnout and striving into a settled, sustainable sense of purpose — so the way they lead and build actually matches who they are.

For Growth in Your Faith

Disciples First Ministries

"Know Jesus, make Jesus known."

Disciples First Ministries is a spiritual home for teaching, Bible study, and pastoral care. It exists to lead believers from discipleship into sonship — helping them understand their identity in Christ, grow in daily relationship with Him, and walk out that identity in their homes, churches, and communities.
