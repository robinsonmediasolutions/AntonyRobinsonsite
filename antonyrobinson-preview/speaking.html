Bio

Antony Robinson is a pastor, author, and speaker who helps Christian entrepreneurs, ministry leaders, and driven professionals move from burnout to alignment. He is the pastor of Disciples First Ministries and the founder of Be More 2 Do More, a personal-growth platform reaching leaders across the faith and business worlds.

He is the author of The Eager Heart: Aligning with Purpose in God's Unseen Plan, and the creator of the Eager Heart Alignment Framework, a methodology built on a single reframe: the shift from "What should I do?" to "Who should I be?" His teaching draws from his own lived history — roughly eleven businesses started between the ages of twenty and thirty-five, most of them failed — and the identity shift that took him from burnout to a sustainable, Christ-centered sense of calling.

Signature Talks

What Antony Speaks On

Each theme is drawn from the same core framework, adapted for your room — church, conference, or company.

01

Who You Are Before What You Do

Why burnout is rarely a productivity problem, and always an identity problem — and how to shift from performing for approval to operating from a settled sense of who you already are in Christ.

02

The Names We Answer To

How false identities — Failure, Imposter, Rejected, Unworthy, Performer — quietly drive our decisions, and how leaders and believers alike can stop responding to a name that was never theirs.

03

Becoming Before Doing

A practical framework for leaders and entrepreneurs who are ready to stop striving for elevation and start building from a place of alignment, character, and calling.
